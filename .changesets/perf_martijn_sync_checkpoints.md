### Decide the static page, the POST-only mutation check, the operation limits and the license check without a copy of the stack

The static page, the check that mutations arrive over POST, the operation limits and the license check each cloned the stack beneath them and boxed a future on every request, though none of their decisions waits on anything. They now decide where the request is called and call the stack beneath in place. No answer changes.
